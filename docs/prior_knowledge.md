# Prior knowledge
## What do you need to know before starting?

Before you start, you should know that MSA-Q is just a tool and not a replacement for specialist knowledge. While 
you should feel free to use it to experiment and learn, if you are planning to use MSA-Q in a professional capacity, 
you (or a collaborator) should have a good grasp of the following scientific concepts:
- The assumptions and limitations of the Pollen Dispersal and Deposition model you are applying
- The assumptions and limitations of the Multiple Scenario Approach.
- The assumptions and limitations of (Relative) pollen productivity estimates (RPPEs) in general, and for the set of values you are planning to use.

In addition, you (or a collaborator) should have some knowledge of:
- The (palaeo-) ecology of the area you are looking to reconstruct
- The (palaeo-) ecology of the species in your reconstruction

And basic skills in the following areas:
- GIS and spatial analysis (for pre- and post processing)
- Data manipulation (for pre- and post-processing)

If you do not have access to this knowledge, or are unsure, check out:
- The scientific publications and references therein on the [publications](publications.md) page.

Happy modelling!
